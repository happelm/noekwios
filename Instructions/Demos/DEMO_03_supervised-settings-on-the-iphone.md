# Demo 3 – Supervised settings on the iPhone

**Module:** M3 Configure supervised devices (after Lab 1.3) · **Duration:** 15 min · **Device:** iPhone 14 (supervised)

> **Draft:** This demo has not been run in this environment yet.

**Goal:** Show what the Lab 1.3 profiles do on a real supervised iPhone.

### Prepare

- Assign selected participant profiles (restrictions, lock screen message, home screen layout, SSO extension, Wi-Fi) to the group of the demo iPhone. Sync the device.

### Show

1. Lock screen: asset tag and footnote from the lock screen message.
2. Home screen: dock and layout from the home screen layout profile.
3. Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → management profile: installed profiles and restrictions.
4. Wi-Fi: the network appears as a managed network.
5. SSO extension: sign in once in Authenticator or a work app, then open Teams or Edge – no second prompt.
6. Remove one assignment, sync, show that the setting disappears.

### Point out

- Many restrictions and device features need supervision – the device restrictions template shows which enrollment type each setting needs.
- Enterprise Wi-Fi with certificates needs a trusted certificate profile plus a SCEP or PKCS profile. If the Wi-Fi profile doesn't arrive, the certificate profiles are the first suspect.
- The Company Portal log on iOS has no information about Wi-Fi, VPN, SCEP or PKCS profiles – see Demo 9.

Sources: Learn – Apple device restriction settings; Apple device feature settings; Troubleshooting Wi-Fi device configuration profiles.
