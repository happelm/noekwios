# Microsoft Intune for iOS/iPadOS – corporate devices

Lab instructions for the two-day workshop on Microsoft Intune for corporate iOS/iPadOS devices: Apple Business, automated device enrollment, configuration, updates, apps, app protection, Conditional Access and operations. Use the copy button on every code block.

> **Lab environment:** one shared demo tenant. Sign in with your `studentX` account (X = your number). Name every object you create with the prefix `studentX`. Real iPhones and iPads are trainer devices – the trainer assigns your policies to them and shows the result.

## Labs

### Day 1 – Get corporate iPhones into Intune

Apple Business and the three tokens, automated device enrollment, configuration of supervised devices, software updates with declarative device management.
